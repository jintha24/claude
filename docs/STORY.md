# The Thief of London — Story Bible (section 5)

**Setting:** London and the surrounding hills, **1866**. A Victorian Robin Hood story.
**Theme:** *"Take from those who won't miss it. Give to those who can't live without it."* (Tobias's rule)
**Tone:** grounded, realistic, no fantasy.

**Harry's arc:** a bitter loner who steals only for himself becomes a legend that all of London's poor
believe in — and then has to decide what kind of legend he'll be.

## Robin Hood → 1866 London

| Classic Robin Hood | Harry's version (1866) |
|---|---|
| Robin Hood, outlaw of Sherwood | **Harry Crane, "The Hill Fox"**, outlaw of the hills outside London |
| Sherwood Forest camp | Harry's **mountain cave**, which grows into the band's hidden camp |
| Prince John | **Lord Edmund Ashcombe** — magistrate, landlord, railway baron, secret underworld boss |
| Sheriff of Nottingham | **Captain Horace Crowe**, corrupt police captain on Ashcombe's payroll |
| Guy of Gisborne | **Nate Colley**, Harry's former best friend, now Ashcombe's enforcer |
| The Merry Men | **The Lantern Men** |
| Little John (bridge fight) | **Big Tom Harlow**, giant dockworker, fought on a narrow footbridge |
| Friar Tuck | **Father Bernard**, slum priest whose soup kitchen hides the money |
| Maid Marian | **Lady Evelyn Marlowe**, Ashcombe's ward, secretly helping the outlaws |
| Much, the miller's son | **Pip**, 12-year-old street kid and lookout |
| Robbing tax collectors | Robbing Ashcombe's **rent coaches and bank carriages** |
| Archery contest in disguise | A Victorian **archery meeting** at Ashcombe's estate |
| Rescue from the gallows | Rescuing Tom from a **public hanging at Newgate** |
| King Richard's pardon | A **Royal Commission from Parliament** |

## Characters

| Character | Role | Who they are |

## Structure

**Prologue — "Tobias's Rule"** (7 years earlier, Harry 19): silver heist at Ashcombe House, Tobias's rule,
a betrayed job, Tobias holds the guards back, Harry escapes over rooftops and on a stolen horse (Cinder).
*Teaches movement, sneaking, climbing, rooftop chase, riding.*

## Endings (Ledger choice + Legend score)
- **The Legend** — Ledger to the Royal Commission. Ashcombe and Crowe arrested; Harry refuses the pardon and returns to the hills.
- **The Outlaw King** — keep the Ledger and blackmail the rich. The Lantern Men leave one by one.
- **The Ghost** — burn the Ledger, give all the gold to the slums, vanish.

Modifiers: spared Nate → he may help in the finale. High Legend → Pip opens a school. Low Legend → people refuse to help in Mission 22.

## 5. Story and Story-Driven Systems (use this exactly)
Setting: London and the surrounding hills, 1866. A Victorian Robin Hood story. Theme: "Take from those who won't miss it. Give to those who can't live without it." Tone: grounded, realistic, no fantasy.

Characters:
- Harry Crane, "The Hill Fox" (26, 6'2"): orphan from the St Giles slums, raised by the "people's thief" Tobias Finch. Has lived alone in a mountain cave for 7 years. Brilliant climber and archer.
- Tobias Finch: mentor, robbed the rich to feed the poor; hanged at Newgate after saving Harry. His hidden letters are collectibles.
- Lord Edmund Ashcombe: main villain (Prince John role); landlord, magistrate, railway baron, secret underworld boss. His crimes are recorded in the "Iron Ledger."
- Captain Horace Crowe: corrupt police captain (Sheriff role) who hunts the Hill Fox.
- Nate Colley: Harry's former best friend who betrayed Tobias; Ashcombe's enforcer (Guy of Gisborne role).
- Inspector Silas Graves: honest Scotland Yard detective, link to the Royal Commission.
- The Lantern Men (Harry's band): Big Tom Harlow (giant dockworker, Little John role, met in a fight on a footbridge), Father Bernard (slum priest, Friar Tuck role), Lady Evelyn Marlowe (Ashcombe's ward, Maid Marian role), Pip (12, street kid and lookout).
- Allies: Mags Doyle (fence), Old Jonah (Thames ferryman), Aldous (hunter who gave Harry his bow). Cinder: Harry's horse.

Structure:
- Prologue "Tobias's Rule": young Harry learns the rule; a heist is betrayed; Tobias is captured; Harry escapes on Cinder.
- Act 1 "The Outlaw of the Hills" (Missions 1-6): cave life and bow hunting, pickpocketing and meeting Pip, returning a widow's rent money, fighting and recruiting Big Tom on a bridge, Father Bernard's church as money hub, newspapers name him "The Hill Fox."
- Act 2 "Steal from the Rich" (Missions 7-13): rent-coach ambush on horseback, recruiting Lady Evelyn, winning an archery contest in disguise, workhouse rescue, bank heist, Nate revealed as the traitor, Ashcombe burns the slums.
- Act 3 "The Price of a Legend" (Missions 14-18): camp raided and Tom captured, the city hides Harry in fog lockdown, rescue Tom from the Newgate gallows, Graves offers a deal with the Royal Commission, band reunites.
- Act 4 "The Last Heist" (Missions 19-22): case Ashcombe Palace, steal the Ledger and gold during the Winter Ball in a snowstorm, rooftop fight with Nate (spare or not), race to Westminster before dawn while the people block the guards.

Endings (Ledger choice plus Legend score): The Legend (give the Ledger to the Royal Commission), The Outlaw King (keep it and blackmail the rich), The Ghost (burn it, give all the gold to the slums, vanish).

Story-driven systems:
- Steal and Give: player splits loot between gear and giving (through Father Bernard); poor districts visibly improve.
- Legend meter: rises from giving, rescues, and non-lethal play; falls from robbing the poor, hurting innocents, or killing. High Legend: citizens hide Harry, warn him, block guards, sing about him in pubs, newspapers print his stories.
- Ashcombe escalation: more theft brings higher rents, more patrols, bigger bounties.
- Outlaw's Code: non-lethal takedowns only.
- Realistic longbow: blunt arrows (knock out lamps and guards), whistle arrows (distraction), rope arrows (climbing lines).
- The Lantern Men: band members give abilities (Tom: distraction and strength, Pip: lookout, Bernard: safehouses, Evelyn: inside info and invitations, Mags: fencing, Jonah: river routes).
- Camp growth: the cave grows from a lonely hideout into a busy outlaw camp.

Side content: Tobias's Letters, rent-coach ambushes, doorstep gifts, archery contests, workhouse rescues, Pip's jobs, Mags's contracts, Aldous's hunts, horse races and jump courses, fishing tournaments, random events (evictions, runaway carriages, fires), wanted posters.

Implement a mission system with objectives, checkpoints, dialogue, in-engine cutscenes, choices, the Legend meter, and ending logic. Build Act 1 first once the core gameplay phases are finished.
