# Phase 7 — Ashcombe House: the First Great Break-in

Lord Ashcombe's town mansion now stands behind its walls east of the market. It's a
full stealth sandbox with several ways in, locked doors, a detector-locked safe,
valuables, patrolling servants, and two mastiffs let off their chains at night.

---

## Part 1 — Getting there

From the market square, look for the gap in the **east row of shops**: that's
**Ashcombe Row**, a short lane leading to the **main gates**. Behind the shops on the
right-hand side runs **the mews**, a back lane along the estate's west wall.

## Part 2 — Ways in (pick your own)

| Route | How | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Front gates** | Open 7 am – 10 pm. Walk in while the gatekeeper looks away, or distract him with a whistle arrow. At night they're locked (a 4-lever lock). | The gatekeeper watches the gate, and the gravel forecourt crunches loudly. |
| **Tradesmen's door** | In the mews. It's locked (3 levers): pick it, or steal its key from the gate lodge. | Leads into the service yard, where a man patrols and the yard dog is chained. |
| **Servants' entrance** | Up the steps from the service yard into the kitchen. Locked at night; the spare key hangs in the lodge. | The kitchen fire lights you up. |
| **Drainpipe to the wing roof** | The pipe on the servants' wing (service-yard side). From the flat roof, climb in through Lady Evelyn's or the linen-room window (the linen room's catch is broken). | You're in plain view of the yard while climbing. |
| **Garden wall** | Take a running jump at any 3.2 m brick wall. | The dogs are loose in the grounds at night. |
| **Garden door and ground-floor windows** | Slip a sash catch (3 s) and climb in, or pick the garden door. | Guards notice windows left open at night. |
| **The sewer** | Lift the heavy **manhole cover in the mews** (it scrapes!), drop in, follow the dark brick tunnel east under the wall, and climb the iron ladder up to a **rusted drain in the south lawn**. | The pitch-dark tunnel is perfectly safe; coming up in the lawn is not. |
| **Portico roof** | From inside, climb out of a front first-floor window onto the portico roof, then drop down. | It's a good escape route. |

## Part 3 — Inside the house

| Floor | Rooms and what's there |
|---|---|
| **Ground (raised)** | Marble entrance hall and grand staircase. **Dining room**: silver candelabra and a silver-gilt epergne on the sideboard. **Library**: an illuminated Book of Hours and Ashcombe's diary. **Butler's pantry** (locked): a canteen of silver cutlery, which rattles as you lift it. |
| **First** | **Picture gallery**: a Landseer and a Dutch still life. Cut them from their frames (hold E for 4 s). **Lord Ashcombe's study** (locked): a gold seal, and the **Chubb safe**. **His bedroom**: the **safe key** on the dressing table, diamond studs and a gold hunter watch. **Lady Evelyn's room**: a pearl necklace and a garnet brooch. **Linen room.** |
| **Servants' wing** | Kitchen with its range, and the servants' hall. |

**The safe** has a genuine **Chubb detector lock** (invented in 1818). You *can* pick its
5 levers, but one slip trips the detector and jams it. After that only the true key will
open it (it resets the detector). The safe holds **£50 in Bank of England notes**, the
rent book of the St Giles tenements, and a letter from Captain Crowe.

## Part 4 — Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **E** (controller: X) | Use what you're facing. The prompt at the bottom of the screen says what that is: open or close a door, unlock it with a key, pick a lock, slip a sash catch, climb through, take something, lift a cover. |
| **Hold E** | For slow jobs (cutting a painting, slipping a catch, lifting a manhole cover). Let go to stop. |
| **Lockpicking** | Harry kneels at the lock. Each lever is a slot; the **brass band** is its gate, and the **light bar** is your pick's tip rising and falling. Press **E** when the tip is inside the gate. A slip drops the last lever and may **snap a pick** (you start with 6). **Move, jump or crouch** to give up. |
| **C while hanging** | Let go (for example, to drop into the sewer shaft). |

## Part 5 — The household and its routine

- **Six of Ashcombe's men** in dark livery, all on duty day and night:
  - Sparrow the gatekeeper, at his post by the lodge
  - one on the forecourt and lawns
  - one in the garden
  - one in the service yard
  - the footman on the ground floor
  - the valet upstairs
- The outdoor men carry **lanterns** after dark.
- **Timetable:**

  | Time | What happens |
  |---|---|
  | 7 am | The gates open. |
  | 5 pm | The gasoliers are lit. |
  | 9 pm | **The dogs are let loose.** |
  | 10 pm | The gates and outer doors are locked. |
  | 11:30 pm | The lamps go out. Only a **night-lamp on the landing** burns. |
  | 6 am | The dogs are chained again. |

- **The front door is always locked**, because the footman answers it.
- **Guards open doors** in their way, and the doors swing shut behind them.
- **Things out of place are noticed:**
  - an outer door or locked room you left open: *"Who left this door open?"*
  - a window left open at night
  - an **empty frame or missing silver**: *"Thieves in the house!"* The alarm is raised and every man comes searching.

### The mastiffs (Brutus and Nell)
- **Their nose:** a dog smells you about 12 m away, up to **twice as far downwind** and only a few metres **upwind**. Watch the chimney smoke to see which way the wind blows. Rain dulls their nose, and so do walls. A hiding place fools them only partly.
- **Barking brings the guards.** A dog that finds you **runs faster than you can sprint** and bites.
- **Climb out of reach** (a wall, a roof) and it will stand beneath you, barking.
- **A blunt arrow** sends it off yelping for a while. It isn't hurt: the Outlaw's Code applies to dogs too.
- **By day** they're chained at their kennels and can't reach you, but they'll still bark.

### Sound and light indoors
- **Creaky floorboards:** the old boards by the study and bedroom doors, at the top of the stairs, in the library and by the pantry. They groan loudly when walked on, but **creep crouched** and they barely whisper.
- **Floors are louder or quieter:** carpet is quiet, floorboards are louder, and the gravel outside is loudest.
- **Rooms are dim by day:** only about 30% of the daylight gets in. A lit **gasolier** lights you up.
- **Closed doors block sight; windows don't.**
- **Hiding places:**
  - **floor-length curtains:** crouch behind them
  - **shrubberies:** crouch in them

## Part 5b — What's on screen

The **interaction prompt** appears at the bottom centre, with a progress bar while you hold E.
The **lock display** shows one slot per lever, which ones are set, and your lockpick
count. Short notes tell you when a pick snaps or a detector trips. Stolen keys and papers
show up in the list at the top right.

**Handy tweaks** (select the node in the Inspector, or edit the constant):
- **Lock difficulty:** `lock_levers` (more levers = more steps) and `lock_gate` (smaller = narrower gate) on any door. For the safe, `levers` and `gate`.
- **Lockpicking skill:** on Harry → *Interaction*:
  - `lock_skill` (above 1 = wider gates)
  - `marker_speed`
  - `pick_break_chance`
- **Dogs:** `smell_range`, `run_speed`, `bite_damage`, `chain_length`.
- **New physics layers:** 7 = glass (blocks bodies and arrows, not sight) and 8 = doors (blocks sight while closed).
